Contributions are accepted under the **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)** — sign off your commits with `git commit -s`. No CLA. By contributing, your code is licensed under Apache 2.0 like the rest of the project. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Relationship to daraja-studio

The companion dashboard, `daraja-studio`, is licensed differently (Business Source License 1.1, source-available, converting to Apache 2.0 on 2030-06-01). **This SDK is and will remain Apache 2.0.** Most developers only consume the SDK and never touch the dashboard licensing.
